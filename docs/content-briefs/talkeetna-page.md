## Verifiable facts

Camp (all from the shipped training page):
- July 21 to 24, 2026. Four days, three nights. Lake near Talkeetna.
- Two workouts a day: morning + late afternoon, drawn from running, roller-skiing,
  strength, hill bounding with poles.
- Midday is the lake: swimming, boats, paddleboards. Evenings end early, mornings start
  early.
- Team cooks dinner together.
- Cabins are dry: outhouses, no showers for four days, the lake is the bath.
- Amy Purevsuren runs camp.
- Free; donations welcome at whatever level works, need-blind.
- Exact site and address go out to registered families. (Mechanism now that registration
  left CrewLAB: the app and/or email. State it plainly without naming the channel.)
- Packing list (verbatim from shipped page): sleeping bag + pillow; training clothes for
  cool and warm; rain gear ("It is Talkeetna."); downtime pants/shorts/sweatshirt; spare
  socks and personal clothing; swimsuit and towel; running shoes; hill bounding poles;
  roller-ski kit if owned (boots, skis, poles, helmet); water bottle + carrier; toiletries;
  daily medications; optional special-diet food; optional personal snacks.
- No practice back in town during camp week.

New mechanics (this pass):
- Camp registration and the waiver are one form at the bottom of this page (`#register`),
  separate from summer-training registration, even for athletes who trained all summer.
- The form asks camp logistics: dietary needs, carpool (needs a ride / can drive and spare
  seats), gear notes.
- Parent or guardian and athlete both sign (typed names); 18+ athletes sign for themselves.
- The signed record is emailed to the family as a copy.
