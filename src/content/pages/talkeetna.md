Camp is July 21 to 24, 2026, four days and three nights at a lake near Talkeetna. We train twice a day, the kids get the lake and the sauna in between, and the team cooks dinner together. It's free, and it registers separately from summer training.

Fair warning on the cabins: they're dry, meaning outhouses and no showers for four days, and the lake is the bath. Amy Purevsuren runs camp. The exact site and address go out to registered families before we drive up.

## A day at camp

The first workout comes in the morning and the second in the late afternoon, drawn from running, roller-skiing, strength, and hill bounding with poles. The middle of the day is the lake: swimming, boats, paddleboards, and the sauna. Evenings end early because the mornings start early.

There is no practice back in town during camp week.

## Getting there

Camp is a drive, and rides are by private car. Tell us in the registration form whether your athlete needs a ride, or whether you can drive and how many spare seats you have.

## What to pack

:::checklist{cols="2"}
#### Sleep

- Sleeping bag and a pillow.

#### Clothing

- Training clothes for cool and warm weather.
- Rain gear. It is Talkeetna.
- Pants, shorts, and a sweatshirt for downtime.
- Spare socks and personal clothing.
- Swimsuit and towel.
- Running shoes.

#### Training gear

- Hill bounding poles.
- Roller-ski kit if you have one: boots, skis, poles, helmet.

#### Daily essentials

- Water bottle, with a carrier for long sessions.
- Toiletries.
- Daily medications.

#### Optional

- Food for special diets.
- Personal snacks.
:::

## Registration

Camp has its own registration, separate from summer sign-up, even if your athlete has been with us all summer. It's the form at the bottom of this page, waiver included. Tell us there about dietary needs, rides, and any gear you need to borrow. A parent and the athlete both sign by typing their names, unless the athlete is already 18 and signs alone. We email you the signed record.

Donations are welcome at whatever level works for your family, and donating or not changes nothing.
