Whew! I just got back from four days of teaching sailing at Big Lake, and finally had a moment to breath and get the Talkeetna Camp registration form completed. It was a lot of fun, but about 12 hours of "on-time" every day - kind of lake State, but much warmer. :-) Oh, and I was lucky enough to get to coach future East runner/skier Adelynn Hilliard's mom and little brother. So cool!)

To register for camp, just use the form on the Talkeetna Camp page here:

[https://ecxc.ski/talkeetna](https://ecxc.ski/talkeetna)

You'll need to check all the appropriate boxes, and digitally sign the form. You need to fill out the form *even if you've already told me or Amy you plan to attend.* And note that we'll be carpooling. When you fill out the form, you can indicate whether you need a ride.

Please fill out the form as soon as you can, so we can have a complete headcount. Coach Amy is running the camp, and she'll only be back from Mongolia for a few days before heading off to camp. It'll help a lot if we know how many athletes will be attending sooner rather than later.

If you have any questions, you can send them via Remind, _or_ use the [contact form](https://ecxc.ski/contact) on the ECXC website. Email will go to both me and Amy.

Thanks, everybody. Looking forward to camp and the rest of this summer's training!
